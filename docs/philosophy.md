# Intent-Driven Development (IDD)

IDD is a philosophy that puts the developer's intent at the center of the AI generation process. 
Instead of fighting with prompt engineering, IDD uses a structured workflow:
1. **Spec**: Ask the right questions to capture intent.
2. **Plan**: Design the technical architecture.
3. **Tasks**: Break down the work into actionable items.
4. **Implement**: Execute sequentially or in parallel.
5. **Review**: Ensure quality against the plan.
